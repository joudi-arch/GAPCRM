# 3D model sources

## Motorcycle

- Title: **Motorcycle**
- Author: **Poly by Google**
- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/5_MTCnqfUTr
- Direct archive used: https://static.poly.pizza/4fa4f21a-c3b8-425f-8e57-9c202b1d6ba6.glb
- License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)
- Retrieved: 2026-06-30
- Modifications: glTF Transform deduplication, pruning, simplification, Meshopt geometry compression, and WebP texture conversion capped at 1024 px. Runtime presentation adds authored paint response, lighting, vibration, headlight, road stage, and narrative motion.

The model is used as a stylized cruiser silhouette. It is not represented as an official Harley-Davidson product model.
